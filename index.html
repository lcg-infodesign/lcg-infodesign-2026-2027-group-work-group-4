PAY DAY

For every country: the average post-tax income of the bottom 50% (lower dot) and of the top 1% (top dot).
Click a country for details.

Methodology

Each line is one country. For the selected year the lower dot is the average post-tax income (USD) of the poorest 50% (avg_posttax_usd_bottom50) and the upper dot is the average post-tax income of the richest 1% (avg_posttax_usd_top1).

  • The vertical axis is logarithmic ($100 to ~$7M): every gridline is 10x the one below, so the length of a line is the ratio between top and bottom, not the dollar gap.
  • 2000-2024, countries grouped by continent, alphabetical.
  • Hover a line for the country name, click it to open the country specific page.
  • Use the left / right arrow keys or the year bar to change year.
