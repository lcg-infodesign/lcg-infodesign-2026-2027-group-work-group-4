PAY DAY

The graph shows the difference in average income between the richest 1% and the poorest 50% of each nation in the world. Hover a bar for details, click it to open the country page.

Methodology

Each bar is one country. For the selected year its height is the difference between the average post-tax income (USD) of the richest 1% (avg_posttax_usd_top1) and of the poorest 50% (avg_posttax_usd_bottom50).

  • The vertical axis is linear but in two stretches: 0 to 1M uses most of the height, 1M to 3M is compressed. Bars above 3M are cut and continue dashed, with their value written on top.
  • 2000-2024, countries grouped by continent, alphabetical.
  • Hover a bar for the exact values, click it to open the country specific page.
  • Use the left / right arrow keys or the year bar to change year.
