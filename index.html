PAY DAY

Each country is a line between two groups: the poorest 50% (filled dot) and the richest 1% (empty circle). The length of the line is the income gap: Income gap = Top 1% − Bottom 50%. Use the two buttons at the top to switch between the income in USD and the multiplier: in the multiplier view the poorest 50% is always 1x and the line shows how many times the top 1% earns more (Top 1% ÷ Bottom 50%). Hover a country for details, click it to open the country page.

the longer the line, the bigger the distance between the two groups

Methodology

Each country is drawn as a line between the average post-tax income (USD) of the poorest 50% (avg_posttax_usd_bottom50, filled dot) and of the richest 1% (avg_posttax_usd_top1, empty circle). The length of the line is the income gap between the two. The multiplier view fixes the poorest 50% at 1x and puts the empty circle at Top 1% ÷ Bottom 50%, i.e. how many times the richest 1% earns the poorest 50%.

  • The vertical axis is linear but in two stretches. In USD: 0 to 1M uses most of the height, 1M to 3M is compressed. In multiplier: 1x to 100x uses most of the height, 100x to 200x is compressed. When the top 1% goes beyond the top of the axis the line is cut and continues dashed, with the value written on top.
  • 2000-2024, countries grouped by continent, alphabetical.
  • Hover a country for the exact values and the income gap, click it to open the country specific page.
  • Use the left / right arrow keys or the year bar to change year.
